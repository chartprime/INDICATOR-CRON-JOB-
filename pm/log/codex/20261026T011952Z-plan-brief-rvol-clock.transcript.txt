# Build brief: Relative Volume by Time of Day

PM brief for the cycle-1 build. Shared rules: PM/WORKER-BRIEF.md. Capability
sheet: PM/CAPABILITIES.md. UX standard: PM/cycle1/ux-standard.md (follow it
where it exists; where it is silent, follow the house conventions in the
existing indicators). Validation protocol: PM/cycle1/validation-protocol.md.

## The trading question

"Is this bar's volume unusual for this time of day, or is it just the open,
the close or the London/New York overlap being its normal self?"

## Target user and decision

Intraday traders on ES, NQ, AAPL, TSLA, BTC and ETH who confirm moves with
volume. Decision: treat participation as genuinely unusual for this minute,
or discount it because this minute is always loud. High relative volume on
its own says nothing about direction or follow-through, and the package says
so. Plain rolling
RVOL fires every morning at the open and goes quiet every lunch; this tool
stays flat where the clock explains the volume and lights up only when the bar
is unusual for its slot.

## Identity

- Folder `apps/web/src/lib/indicators/relative-volume/`, def `relativeVolume.ts`,
  computation in `relativeVolumeMath.ts` (pure, testable), tests in `tests/`.
- id `relativeVolume`, label `"Relative Volume (Time of Day)"`, category
  `"technical"`, placement `{ kind: "ownPane", priceLines: [1, 2, 3] }`.
- Historical Mode policy `OHLCV_POLICY`. Works on every archived instrument
  and on equities (unsided volume is irrelevant: only `bar.volume` is read).
- `timeframeGate`: active for `interval_ms <= 3_600_000` (one hour and below).
  Daily and above have no time of day.

## Data

`bar.ts_open`, `bar.interval_ms`, `bar.volume`, `bar.closed`, `bar.provider`,
`bar.assetClass`, `bar.marketKind`. Nothing else. No wall clock. Identity
fields choose the clock convention only; availability is governed by the
OHLCV capability policy, never by provider. A bar whose `volume` is not a
finite number >= 0 contributes no sample and yields `null` for that bar.
Missing bars (gaps in `ts_open`) contribute nothing, never a zero sample.

Math library note: the `ta.*` factories are top-level imports
(`import { ta } from "../../chart/ta"`) created once inside `createState`,
exactly as `docs/architecture/indicator-sdk/TA.02-ta-math-library-evidence.md`
describes; `ctx.ta` is an intentionally empty namespace and is not used. The
rolling comparison uses `ta.sma(20)` fed with committed volumes only (see the
commit rule), so its denominator excludes the current bar, mirroring the slot
method; `null` until 20 committed bars.

## Calculation (incremental, one bar at a time)

1. **Clock.** Equities and futures (provider DXFEED or DATABENTO): New York
   local minute of day, computed the way `killzones` does it (reuse
   `apps/web/src/lib/chart/marketCalendar.ts` or the `nyParts` helper; do not
   write a new time-zone routine). Crypto (provider BINANCE): UTC minute of
   day, with two slot families, weekday and weekend, selected by the UTC day of
   week. Advanced setting `clock`: `auto` (default), `newYork`, `utc`.
2. **Slot.** `slot = floor(minuteOfDay / barMinutes)` for intraday bars; for
   `interval_ms >= 3_600_000` the slot is the hour. Slot count is bounded by
   1,440 per family.
3. **Baseline.** Per slot, a ring buffer of the last `sessions` committed
   same-slot volumes (default 10, range 2..60) and their median. Median, not
   mean, so one news day does not inflate tomorrow's baseline.
4. **Baseline read before write.** For the bar being evaluated, the slot
   median and the rolling SMA are read first (`baselineAtOpen`), stored on the
   bar's own record, and every later recomputation of that bar (a `replace`
   revision, a duplicate closed dispatch) divides by that stored value. A bar
   never sees its own volume in its denominator.
5. **Commit rule (one insertion per `ts_open`).** State keeps the current bar
   record `{ts_open, volume, baselineAtOpen, committed}`. The bar's volume is
   inserted into its slot ring and into the rolling SMA exactly once, at the
   first of these events: (a) the first dispatch of that `ts_open` with
   `bar.closed === true`; (b) the arrival of a bar with a greater `ts_open`
   while the current record is still uncommitted (a close that never arrived,
   for example across a feed gap), in which case its last known volume is
   inserted. After insertion `committed = true`; later dispatches of the same
   `ts_open` (duplicate close, `replace`) recompute that bar's ratio from its
   stored `baselineAtOpen` and do not insert again. A dispatch with a
   `ts_open` older than the current record is ignored. During a full replay
   every bar is `closed`, so every bar commits on its own dispatch, which is
   why replay and live agree.
6. **Confirmation.** A bar's outputs and markers are provisional while
   `bar.closed === false` (drawn lighter) and confirmed on the first closed
   dispatch, including the last bar of a replay. No bar ever emits a marker
   for a different bar: a marker is always for the bar of the current call,
   so the runtime's per-timestamp replace cannot leave stale or duplicate
   markers.
7. **Output.** `rvol = bar.volume / slotMedian`, `null` while the slot has
   fewer than `minSessions` (default 3) samples or the median is 0 or not
   finite. Median of an even count is the mean of the two middle values. The
   forming bar is divided by the full-slot expectation, so it grows toward its
   final value during the bar. `rolling = bar.volume / sma20` of committed
   volumes, `null` during its warm-up. `minSessions` is clamped to
   `<= sessions` at read time.
8. **Markers (alert conditions).** `hot`: `rvol >= hotThreshold` (default 2)
   draws a small circle on that bar; `rvol >= 3` the same circle, larger.
   `sustained` (advanced, off by default): the third consecutive bar with
   `rvol >= sustainedLevel` draws one marker, once per run; the run re-arms
   when a bar falls below the level, when a slot gap (a bar missing from the
   sequence) occurs, or at a session boundary. Markers are derived from state
   on every call, so a replace redraws rather than duplicates.
9. **Sessions, slots and duration.** A ring holds observations, one per
   committed bar; because a slot occurs once per calendar session, a session
   contributes at most one sample per slot. Exception: the repeated New York
   hour at the autumn DST change can land two futures bars in one slot; both
   are kept as observations, documented, no special case. Bars shorter than
   the interval (the 15:30 to 16:00 equity hour bar, an early close) are
   compared with the same slot on other sessions, so like is compared with
   like; an early-close session's short last bar is one observation among
   `sessions` and the median absorbs it. `sessions` is the ring capacity, not
   a promise of available history: retained history supplies what it has, and
   the table shows the actual sample count.
10. **No future data.** Every value at bar t uses only bars with
    `ts_open <= t.ts_open`. Truncating the series at any index must not change
    earlier outputs or earlier markers (the validator checks both).
11. **Fail closed.** Any non-finite intermediate yields `null`.

## Baseline to beat, and the evidence required

Baseline: `volume / SMA(20)` of the previous 20 committed bars (the
comparison line itself). Both methods are evaluated at the same threshold
(2.0), on the same bars (those where both are ready), and both recall and
false-positive rate are reported for both without a predetermined winner.

Deterministic fixture (seeded, in the validation harness): 15 equity
sessions of 390 one-minute bars. Expected volume per slot is a U curve:
4.0x the midday level over the first 10 minutes decaying to 1.0x by minute
60, flat 1.0x until minute 330, rising to 2.0x at the close. Noise:
multiplicative log-normal with sigma 0.15 from a fixed seed. Anomalies: 12
planted bars in sessions 4 to 15, slots drawn with the seed from minutes 90
to 330, at 2.5x that slot's expected volume. Evaluation bars: sessions 4 to
15. "Predictable slots" are the first and last 10 minutes of each session.

Acceptance for this indicator (descriptive, not predictive):
- Recall on the 12 anomalies >= 11/12.
- False-positive rate on predictable slots <= 10 percent of those bars.
- The rolling method's recall and false-positive rate on the same bars are
  reported beside these in the doc page, whatever they are. The hypothesis
  under test is that the time-of-day method has a lower false-positive rate
  at the open and close with comparable recall; if the fixture disproves it,
  the brief returns to the PM.

Additional fixtures the validator must run: a weekend-split crypto series

# Data for indicator authors

[Handbook](../../README.md) · [Indicator index](README.md) ·
[Onboarding](../../operations/00-developer-onboarding.md) ·
[Indicator SDK](../indicator-sdk/TA.01-indicator-authoring-core-evidence.md)

An indicator never opens SQLite, S3 or a provider connection itself. It reads
what the runtime and the archive deliver through a small number of supported
boundaries. This page names those boundaries, says what each one carries for
each instrument, and lists the rules every indicator has to follow. The code
named here is the authority; when this page and the code disagree, the code
wins and this page is the defect.

## Three sources of data

| Source | How it reaches an indicator | You need | When it is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live ticks** | Terminal C holds the provider session and sends canonical frames (`bar`, `trade`, `book`, `health`, profiles, VPIN readings) over the local `/ws` WebSocket | Binance: nothing. dxFeed: layer 2 (Tailscale broker) | dxFeed instruments show *History only*: with layer 1 their recorded host history still loads, without new ticks |
| **Warm-up** | Before live ticks arrive, the runtime reads recent history through its storage reader: candles, large trades, aggressive prints, volume profile, VPIN calibration | Layer 1 (WireGuard + bearer); otherwise only a local collector's SQLite | The indicator starts from live ticks only and reports itself not ready |
| **Archive** | Historical Mode reads candles through `/archive/query` and asks the host for four studies through `/archive/study` | Layer 1 | Historical Mode shows *Historical Mode needs host data access* |

The three layers are explained in [onboarding](../../operations/00-developer-onboarding.md);
`npm run doctor` reports which ones a machine has.

Warm-up and live ticks meet in the runtime; the archive is a separate path used
only by Historical Mode. A successful warm-up does not prove an archive
partition exists, and an archive answer says nothing about the live collector
today.

Live Mode also has a bounded `band_history` request for a historical price band.
The laptop sends the instrument, price range and time window through Terminal C;
the host replays its recorded raw depth and returns resting size and traded
flow per row. Institutional Order Blocks v1 reads a block the visible live
history did not cover from the host's stored heatmap columns first
(`/heatmap/columns`, cut to the block's rows in the browser) and uses
`band_history` only when the host has no stored columns for the pair or that
read fails. Session Levels reads a touched level's five minutes and a range's
totals from the same stored columns, with no raw-replay fallback. A missing book interval remains a gap, never a
zero-liquidity observation. Each host read is capped at two hours, 256 rows and
100,000 row/column cells; the client pieces longer requests. This is an
on-demand derivation, **not a stored canonical L2 row dataset**, and it does
not make order-book indicators available in the separate Historical Mode
archive. See the [wire contract](../../contracts/ws-frame-contract/v1/ws-frame-contract-v1.md)
and `apps/runtime/src/server/band-history.js` for the exact boundary.

## What each instrument has

| Instrument | Live ticks | Warm-up from the host | Historical candles | Historical studies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC, ETH spot and perpetual (Binance) | always | yes | 1m, 1h, 1d, each with taker buy volume | none yet |
| ES, NQ (continuous futures) | with layer 2 | yes, what the weekday collector captured | 1m, 1h, 1d | profile, VPIN, large prints, bar flow |
| AAPL, TSLA (equities) | with layer 2 | yes, what the weekday collector captured | 1m, 1h, 1d | profile, VPIN, large prints, bar flow |
| NVDA (equity) | with layer 2 | yes, what the weekday collector captured | not archived | none |

**Equity candles are regular session only**, live and historical alike:
09:30–16:00 New York time, with the exchange's holidays and early closes from
[`config/market-calendar.json`](../../../config/market-calendar.json). Hourly
bars start at 09:30, the last one ends at the bell, and a daily bar is that
session. Pre-market and after-hours prints are not in equity candles. Futures
and crypto candles are UTC-aligned and cover every traded minute.

Other timeframes are resampled in the browser from those rungs. The governed
instrument list, exact provider symbols and tick sizes live in
[`config/market-instruments.json`](../../../config/market-instruments.json);
the archive's list lives in `apps/web/src/lib/archive/historicalCatalog.ts`.

The four studies are computed on the host from trade-derived families, so the
browser never pages minute rows. Their request, limits and refusals are in the
[archive study contract](../../contracts/archive-study/v1/archive-study-v1.md).
In short: a profile per requested range, VPIN over whole UTC weeks (at most 26),
and large prints and bar flow over one bar-aligned range of at most 5,000 bars.

## Which indicators work in Historical Mode

`HISTORICAL_INDICATOR_POLICIES` in `apps/web/src/lib/chart/indicatorRegistry.ts`
decides this, per indicator, from the capabilities the selected archive
provides. There are three policies:

| Policy | Needs | Works on |
|---|---|---|
| Candles | `ohlcv` | every archived instrument |
| Candle flow | `ohlcv` + `buy_volume` | Binance (candles carry taker buy volume) and ES/NQ/AAPL/TSLA (the `bar_flow` study supplies both sides) |
| Trade families | `ohlcv` + `trade_families` | ES/NQ/AAPL/TSLA |

An indicator can also be marked unavailable with a reason, as order-book and
every-trade indicators are: the archive has no historical depth and keeps only
the largest prints of each minute.

## Rules every indicator follows

**Read buy and sell through `barSides`.** Never compute sell volume as
`volume - buy_volume`. `apps/web/src/lib/indicators/barSides.ts` returns
`{known, buy, sell, unknown, delta, sided}` for a bar. It uses an explicit
`sell_volume` when the bar has one, and falls back to `volume - buy_volume`
only for bars that carry just a buy side. That fallback is exact for Binance,
where every trade has a taker side. It is not exact for equities.

**Unsided volume is real and large for equities.** A Databento equity trade
often has no aggressor side. Measured on the host (Report 54): ES and NQ are
effectively fully sided, but AAPL is about 40% unsided on one-minute bars and
65–75% on hourly and daily bars; TSLA is 35–55%. Historical bars carry that
volume separately as `unknown`, and `unsidedShare(bars)` gives the share to
show beside an equity delta. Counting it as selling would make every equity
delta read bearish by that amount.

Live dxFeed bars built from the tape carry buy and sell only when every trade in
that minute had a side. A minute with any unsided trade carries neither, so
`barSides` reports it unknown and it draws no delta, which is common for
equities. Stored native dxFeed candles carry both sides explicitly (volume at
the ask and at the bid), with any remainder unknown.

**`missing` is not `empty`.** A study reports ranges it could not read as
`missing`; draw them as unavailable, never as zero. It reports ranges it read
and found no trades in (a weekend, an overnight hour for an equity) as `empty`,
and those legitimately have nothing to draw. A bar with no trades is absent
from `bar_flow`; draw no delta for it, not a zero delta.

**Gate on capabilities, not on provider names.** Declare what the indicator
needs through its `HISTORICAL_INDICATOR_POLICIES` entry. Do not branch on
`provider === "BINANCE"`. The map is typed over every indicator id, so a new
indicator does not compile until it has a policy.

**Warm-up failure is not an empty signal.** When the storage reader cannot
reach the host it reports the read as unavailable. Show the indicator as
warming up or degraded, never as a flat or zero reading.

**Quantities are provider units.** Futures sizes are contracts, equity sizes
are shares, Binance sizes are the base asset. Do not add them across providers
or convert them to a notional the provider did not report.

**Need a new trade-derived aggregate?** Do not fetch minute rows into the
browser. A new study is a change to the archive study contract and the host,
and goes through the owner.

## Adding an indicator that works in both modes

1. Create `apps/web/src/lib/indicators/<name>/`. The folder owns the whole
   indicator: computation, geometry, primitive and settings. Follow the
   [SDK execution model](../indicator-sdk/TA.01-indicator-authoring-core-evidence.md).
   Its settings dialog gets [setting presets](setting-presets.md): a
   `settingsSchema` is enough for the generic dialog, and a hand-built one
   declares a preset schema for every setting it stores.
2. Register it in `INDICATOR_REGISTRY` and give it a
   `HISTORICAL_INDICATOR_POLICIES` entry that names what it needs.
3. Read sides through `barSides`. For example, a cumulative delta that leaves
   bars without a known side undrawn:

   ```ts
   import type { BarFrame } from "../../../types/frames";
   import { barSides } from "../barSides";

   export function cumulativeDelta(bars: readonly BarFrame[]): (number | null)[] {
     let total = 0;
     return bars.map((bar) => {
       const sides = barSides(bar);
       if (!sides.known) return null;
       total += sides.delta;
       return total;
     });
   }
   ```

4. Check it in Live Mode on Binance (every machine can), then in Historical
   Mode on BTC and on one futures and one equity instrument (layer 1). On AAPL,
   confirm the unsided share is shown and the delta is not skewed.
5. Add tests for the computation, lifecycle, capability gating and rendering,
   as listed under *Adding or changing an indicator* in the
   [indicator index](README.md).

export function equitySessionAt(tsMs: number): EquitySession {
  const { date, weekday, minute } = localParts(tsMs);
  if (!WEEKDAYS.has(weekday) || equity.holidays.has(date)) return "CLOSED";
  if (minute < equity.extendedOpen || minute >= equity.extendedClose) return "CLOSED";
  const close = equity.earlyCloses.has(date) ? equity.earlyClose : equity.regularClose;
  return minute >= equity.regularOpen && minute < close ? "REGULAR" : "EXTENDED";
}

export function futureSessionOpenAt(tsMs: number): boolean {
  const { weekday, minute } = localParts(tsMs);
  if (weekday === "Sat") return false;
  if (weekday === "Sun") return minute >= futureOpen;
  if (weekday === "Fri") return minute < futureClose;
  return minute < futureClose || minute >= futureOpen;
}

export function regularSessionAt(tsMs: number): { openMs: number; closeMs: number } | null {
  if (equitySessionAt(tsMs) !== "REGULAR") return null;
  const { date, minute } = localParts(tsMs);
  const openMs = Math.floor(tsMs / MINUTE_MS) * MINUTE_MS - (minute - equity.regularOpen) * MINUTE_MS;

  return { openMs, closeMs: openMs + (close - equity.regularOpen) * MINUTE_MS };
}

/**
 * Commits this bar's draw.fill/markers.plotshape/plotchar/table.cell calls,
 * once per bar-transition (never once per call inside onBar) -- the same
 * discipline pushOutput/overwriteLastOutput already give the numeric line
 * channel, extended to the per-bar declarative draw channels.
 */
function commitDrawFlush(
  record: ActivationRecord,
  bar: BarFrame,
  replace: boolean,
): void {
  for (const [key, points] of record.fillPointsByKey) {
    const point = record.pendingFillPoints.get(key) ?? null;
    if (replace) overwriteLastFillPoint(points, point);
    else pushFillPoint(points, point);
  }
  // A band first filled on this bar has no history: backfill it with the bars
  // it did not exist for, so every band's points stay one per bar.
  for (const key of record.pendingFillPoints.keys()) {
    if (record.fillPointsByKey.has(key)) continue;
    // On a re-delivery of the newest bar that bar is already counted, so it takes the band's first point.
    const points: (FillPoint | null)[] = new Array(replace ? Math.max(0, record.fillBars - 1) : record.fillBars).fill(null);
    points.push(record.pendingFillPoints.get(key) ?? null);
    record.fillPointsByKey.set(key, points);
  }
  if (!replace) record.fillBars += 1;
  if (replace) {
    overwriteBarMarkerCalls(
      record.markerCalls,
      bar.ts_open,
      record.pendingMarkerCalls,
    );
  } else {
    pushMarkerCalls(record.markerCalls, record.pendingMarkerCalls);
  }
  flushTableCells(record.drawOwnerId);
}

function lowerBoundByOpenTime(bars: readonly BarFrame[], tsOpen: number): number {
  let low = 0;
  let high = bars.length;
  while (low < high) {
    const middle = low + Math.floor((high - low) / 2);
    if (bars[middle].ts_open < tsOpen) low = middle + 1;
    else high = middle;
  }
  return low;
}

export function replaceClosedBars(bars: readonly BarFrame[]): BarFrame[] {
  const byOpenTime = new Map<number, BarFrame>();
  for (const bar of bars) {
    if (bar.closed) byOpenTime.set(bar.ts_open, bar);
  }
  return [...byOpenTime.values()]
    .sort((a, b) => a.ts_open - b.ts_open)
    .slice(-MAX_RETAINED_SOURCE_BARS);
}

export function upsertClosedBar(bars: BarFrame[], bar: BarFrame): void {
  if (!bar.closed) throw new Error("upsertClosedBar: bar must be finalized");
  const last = bars[bars.length - 1];
  if (last === undefined || last.ts_open < bar.ts_open) bars.push(bar);
  else {
    const index = lowerBoundByOpenTime(bars, bar.ts_open);
    if (bars[index]?.ts_open === bar.ts_open) bars[index] = bar;
    else bars.splice(index, 0, bar);
  }
  if (bars.length > MAX_RETAINED_SOURCE_BARS) {
    bars.splice(0, bars.length - MAX_RETAINED_SOURCE_BARS);
  }
}

  closedBars: BarFrame[],
  developingBar: BarFrame | null,
  bar: BarFrame,

1. **Specific trading question: yes.** “Is this volume unusual for this time of day?” supports a concrete participation check. Keep the direction/follow-through limitation. Replace “stays flat” with “normalizes recurring intraday volume patterns”; noise, regime changes, and limited samples still move the ratio.

2. **Calculation: causal, but not fully specified or replay-equivalent yet.**

   - **Corrections after commitment break equivalence.** Suppose a bar closes at volume 100, then receives a closed replacement at 150. Live state retains 100 in its ring/SMA, while replay inserts 150. Subsequent denominators differ. Specify correction handling that updates the committed observation or triggers reconstruction, while preserving the corrected bar’s pre-insertion denominator. Test a *changed-volume* closed replacement, not just an identical duplicate.
   - **Retention creates another divergence.** Live rings retain observations after their source bars leave chart retention; `REBUILT` creates fresh state from retained bars. With seven crypto days and a ten-observation lookback, those baselines can differ. Define whether retained history or longer-lived state is authoritative, then require equivalence under that explicit policy.
   - **Short equity bars can enter the wrong slot.** The resampler uses actual shortened `interval_ms`. A 15:30–16:00 hourly candle therefore has a 30-minute interval: the stated formula assigns slot 31, while regular hourly bars use hour slots. This collides with the ordinary 15:00 hourly slot. Use a stable clock-start key and explicitly handle duration differences; do not claim early-close bars necessarily compare like with like.
   - **Sustained markers need transactional state.** Specify committed run state plus a provisional current-bar calculation so repeated forming updates cannot advance the run. Resolve “third consecutive bar” versus configurable `sustainedBars`, define session boundaries, and state how null/invalid bars affect the run.
   - The runtime preserves supplied `closed` flags during replay; it does not make every replay bar closed. Correct that assumption and test replay with a forming tail.

3. **Data availability: mostly sound.** The listed fields exist, total volume needs no sided-volume data, and `OHLCV_POLICY` is appropriate. Missing-volume and missing-bar behavior is honest.

   **Default one-minute crypto weekends remain learning after every fresh seven-day replay:** only two weekend observations per slot are available, below `minSessions = 3`. This is a persistent coverage limitation, not merely a fresh-symbol warm-up. Change the default split/minimum, obtain sufficient supported history, or explicitly accept and prominently describe that behavior.

   Also define invalid-input precedence: an invalid current volume should report unavailable even when the slot is still learning.

4. **Default UX: understandable, with two fixes.** Three primary controls are appropriate, and hiding the rolling line keeps the view focused.

   - Show **“Forming — versus full-bar expectation”** beside the provisional value; lighter styling alone does not explain why an ordinary developing bar starts low.
   - The specified table omits the actual sample count promised elsewhere. Include it.
   - `timeframeGate` excludes the indicator from activation in ChartPanel, so an indicator-owned table cannot display the proposed gated status. Specify a host-level message or revise the gating/display approach.

5. **Validation: substantially testable; benchmark is fair for its stated narrow hypothesis.** Same bars, readiness, and threshold make the rolling comparison reasonable. However:

   - Freeze the PRNG, seed, exact U-curve formula, anomaly placement/uniqueness, and whether anomalies multiply expected volume or noisy volume.
   - Define false positives as detections on **non-anomaly eligible bars**, and report overall false-positive rate alongside predictable-slot results.
   - Fifteen × 390 bars is **5,850**, not the specified 10,080-bar performance replay. Supply a longer deterministic fixture and an explicit rollover/rebuild comparison.
   - Fix the lifecycle contradictions above before requiring unconditional live/replay equivalence. Add expected outcomes to the DST, gap, early-close, and changed-close fixtures.

6. **Simplest alternative:** a one-minute-only version using the median of prior observations at the exact clock minute, one RVOL line, one threshold, and hot markers. Defer weekend splitting, sustained markers, and the rolling display; retain rolling RVOL in validation. This answers most of the question while removing several lifecycle and duration complications.
