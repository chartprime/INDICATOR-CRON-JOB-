# Aggressive Orders Profile

The big market orders of the candles on screen in a pane under the chart: one bar per candle, as a net delta (the big buys less the big sells, in the buy colour from the zero line up and the sell colour down) or as buys and sells (the buys up on one series, the sells down on another), a gap where no big print traded; and bars at or over a multiple of the average bar on screen kept at full colour while the rest are dimmed. The pane's name sits on a small chip at the bottom-left of the pane directly above it, normally the price chart, so it never covers the bars. Scroll or zoom and it reads the range again once the chart has stood still for 300 ms. The forming candle's bar is live: every big print that lands in it moves the bar at once, easing from where it stood to the new delta over `LIVE_EASE_MS` with a pulse that brightens the bar and fades over `LIVE_PULSE_MS`, swapping colour when the delta crosses zero. Indicator id `aggressiveOrdersProfile`, category L2, not offered on historical charts.

A print counts when its size is at or above the floor and its own time falls inside the visible range and inside a candle on the chart. The floor starts at the heatmap's: the heatmap columns' median print floor rounded to the nearest 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten (`startingPrintFloorFor`: the fine columns, the deep columns when the fine ones hold no prints, as after a restart with the market shut, and the instrument's suggested floor with no columns at all), which the print store takes at its first want and keeps for the pair's session, so it is the lowest floor anything is loaded at. That first want waits for the heatmap's columns, at most `START_FLOOR_WAIT_MS` (5 s) after the store appears, so a pair switch, whose first columns can reach the chart after the new store, never starts the store from the instrument's suggested floor. The Big print floor slider only goes up from there, on stops spaced by ratio up to the instrument's top (12, 15, 20 ... 40, 50 BTC above a 10 BTC heatmap floor), and every stop is a subset of what the store holds, so raising the floor redraws at once and never loads again; a saved floor at or under the heatmap's reads as the heatmap's. Two lines under the slider say where it starts and that Aggressive Orders draws its bubbles from its own threshold, so setting the same floor makes each bar the sum of its candle's bubbles over the last 4 hours; past that Aggressive Orders keeps only its 4 biggest prints a minute a side, so an older candle's bar can outweigh its bubbles.

## Files

- `rangePrintStore.ts` — the big prints of the range, complete, because the heatmap's columns keep only their own moving top-2 % of prints and at most 64 per coarse column. `RangePrintStore`, one per pair in `useMarketData`, follows `DeltaProfileStore`: a cut just after the newest print seen when first wanted, or the clock when none was seen, in which case it asks the recording only once the feed has passed the cut, so a lagging feed's late prints come from the recording; live prints at or above the floor from the cut on (`addTrade`, fed every live trade); before the cut, the runtime's recording through the existing `aggressive_history` request (`nextRequest` / `put`), only once the recorder has had `RANGE_ASK_DELAY_MS` past the cut, what the focus (the candles on screen) leaves open first, newest first, then the stretch after the focus from its near end, then the stretch before it from its near end (`nextAsk`), `RANGE_ASK_MAX_MS` (6 h) at most per request, following the cursor of a capped reply and laying the pages in as one piece. One request at a time, and at most `RANGE_ASK_BUDGET` (40) in any `RANGE_ASK_BUDGET_MS` (60 s), so Aggressive Orders keeps room in the host's 60 a minute per connection; `dropWant` (the indicator off or hidden) stops the asks and keeps the recording; a busy host's `retry_after_ms` costs nothing, a failed read backs off, three failures give the stretch up for `RANGE_GIVE_UP_MS`, as does a reply the host could not convert or more than `MAX_PAGES_PER_ASK` pages. The store takes its floor from its first want and keeps it for good: a read at or above it filters print by print, a read under it gets nothing and the whole range as a gap. A reconnect (`reanchor`) drops the live prints and the request but keeps the recording. Bounded: `MAX_LIVE_PRINTS` (the older half is handed over to the recording past it) and `MAX_RECORDED_PRINTS` (pieces farthest from the window go first; when the window alone needs more it stops asking, checked again whenever the window moves, so the week sliding with the clock never opens it again). `coverage(from, to, floor, now)` gives the gaps nothing covers and whether it will still fill them (`loading`), without walking a print; `read(from, to, floor, now)` adds the counts; `readCandles(from, to, floor, candles, now)` gives the same by candle (`candleAt`: the candle whose open is the last at or before the print's time, if the print falls before its close; a print before the first candle or in a hole between candles counts nothing), walking a recorded piece and the candles together since both run in time order. `subscribe` tells the chart when the recording changes, never per live print; `subscribeLive` hands each live print the store keeps (its time, size and side) to the pane's live bar. Its ids come from the counter Aggressive Orders uses (`nextAggressiveHistoryRequestId`). A stretch the recording itself lacks (the collector down while the runtime ran) comes back as a complete, empty answer, so it reads as no big print and no legend can say otherwise: the reply does not say how far the recording reaches.
- `aggressiveProfileReader.ts` — the pure read. `readAggressiveProfile` takes the store's candles and reads the heatmap's own columns only over the gaps the store reports, or over the whole range with no store (`readColumnCandles`): a print counts by its own time, so a column up to `PRINT_WINDOW_COLUMNS` windows after a gap and one window before it is read for the prints it carries from inside the gap and nothing else; each tier answers only for its own stretch of time (`PrintTier`), so a print two tiers carry, or one a column carries late, counts once, with no lookup; at most `MAX_PRINT_COLUMNS` columns, the newest first; floor 0 takes each column's own floor. `candlesOf` lists the candles oldest first, each with its delta (`CandleDelta`). `niceFloor`, `autoPrintFloorOf` and `printFloorFor` are the auto floor.
- `aggressiveProfileSource.ts` — `AggressiveProfileSource(grid, printsOf)` is the reader's source. `read(range, settings, candles, now)` keys on the grid's version (only while the store's `coverage` leaves a gap in the range, since the columns fill only gaps: with the whole range held, a new heatmap column changes nothing and nothing is read again, the forming candle's bar moving with the live prints in between), the grid's epoch, the range, the resolved floor, the store's version and the candles (their count, their first and last opens, their length); the first read happens at once, a changed key is read again only once it has stood still for `READ_SETTLE_MS`, and `settleAt` says when to come back. It reads at the higher of the hand floor and the store's kept floor once the store has one; before that at the hand floor, or the heatmap's auto floor resolved once per grid version. `printTiersOf` lists the print sources: the fine columns from the start of their first window on, the deep columns for everything before it.
- `aggressiveProfilePrimitive.ts` — `AggressiveProfilePrimitive`, a series primitive on the price series that draws nothing: on every chart update it takes the time scale's visible range, from the first visible bar's open to the last visible bar's close, clamped to the bars (`visibleRangeOf`; the time scale names bars by their open, so the newest candle's whole minute counts while it is in view), reads through its source for the chart's bars, and hands what it read to `onRead` with the range and its settings, once per read (again after a settings change or a `repaint`), `null` when nothing is in view or the source goes. It comes back on its own when the source says to (one timer, dropped when the read settles, the source changes or it is detached).
- `aggressiveProfilePane.ts` — `AggressiveProfilePane` owns two histograms in its own pane under the chart (`attach` adds them at the end of the pane order, the dotted zero line on the first; `detach` removes them); `render` sets their points from `panePoints`: one per candle inside the range, the delta coloured by its sign, or the buys on the first histogram and the sells below zero on the second, whitespace for a candle with no big print, and every bar under `outlierThreshold` (the average bar with prints on screen, in the view shown, times the Highlight setting; nothing at zero) dimmed to `DIM_ALPHA` by `barColour`. The pane's name is a `PaneTagPrimitive` attached to the pane directly above the profile's pane; `placeTag` checks which pane that is now and moves the chip, on every render and whenever ChartPanel's indicator set changes, so removing the pane above never takes the chip with it. `render` also notes the forming candle (the chart's last bar, while the view is open and holds it) with what the read gave it; `pushLive` takes each live print the store keeps, adds its size to that candle's buys or sells when its time falls inside it, and runs animation frames (`tick`) that `update` the forming candle's bar alone: eased from where it stood (`tweened`, each side on its own), coloured by the sign or the side, dimmed under the threshold and brightened by the pulse at the bar's own alpha, so a dimmed bar stays dimmed (`lighten`, `pulseAt`), until the bar rests and the pulse is gone. A new read of the same candle retargets from where the bar stands; the same read shown again (a repaint, a change of look) keeps the live prints; the bar rests exactly on its target; a read the chart makes from inside a frame never starts a second loop; a new candle, a closed view or a detach drops the live bar and its frames.
- `paneTagPrimitive.ts` — `PaneTagPrimitive`, a pane primitive that draws the pane's name on a small chip (`PANE_TAG_*`: 10 px text, 16 px tall, 8 px from the left, 4 px above the bottom) at the bottom-left of the pane it is attached to, in the chart's font, over everything else there; nothing in a pane too short to hold it.
- `aggressiveProfileSettings.ts` — per-instrument settings saved with the live-indicator extras (`aggressiveProfileByInstrument`): big print floor (0 = auto, the heatmap's floor, the store's once it has one; `floorScaleFor` tops the slider at 50 BTC, 1,000 ETH, 100 NQ contracts, 300 ES contracts or 2,000 shares, or at `FLOOR_TOP_REACH` (10) times the auto floor rounded up to 1-2-5 once the auto floor reaches that top, whole units on futures and equities, and elsewhere at `FALLBACK_FLOOR_SPAN` (100) times the auto floor rounded up to 1-2-5, `FALLBACK_FLOOR_MAX` (100) with none; `floorStops` spaces its stops by ratio, `FLOOR_MANTISSAS` (1, 1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8) times each power of ten from `FLOOR_DECADES` (3) decades under the top, or from one whole unit, strictly above the floor it starts from, up to the top; `floorPosition` and `floorAt` turn a floor into the nearest stop and back), the view (`profileView`: net delta or buys and sells), the highlight multiple (`outlierFrom`, 0 to `MAX_OUTLIER_FROM` in steps of `OUTLIER_STEP`, 0 = off), buy and sell colours (start on the heatmap's bid and ask colours); the schema the presets read; `readerSettingsOf` hands the reader only the floor, so a colour, the view or the highlight never reads the range again; `sizeText`, `floorNumberText` and `outlierText` for the labels. An entry saved before the view and the highlight existed gets their defaults on load (`ADDED_LATER` in `liveIndicatorExtras`).
- `AggressiveProfileSettingsModal.tsx` — the dialog on the shared `IndicatorSettingsModal` with presets. Inputs: Big print floor (in the book's units, "auto (the heatmap's, N BTC now)" at 0, the stops above it, and two lines under it: "Starts at the heatmap's floor, N BTC." and "Aggressive Orders draws its bubbles from another threshold; set the same floor to make each bar the sum of its candle's bubbles over the last 4 hours (older minutes keep only its 4 biggest prints a side)."), Show as (Net delta, Buys and sells), Highlight bars over N× the average (off at 0). Visibility: Buys color, Sells color, Show on chart.
- `aggressiveOrdersProfileIndicator.ts` — the menu entry.

`ChartPanel` owns the primitive and the pane and builds the source over the grid MainPanel hands it (`applyAggressiveProfile`, which lets go of a cached source as soon as the grid changes or goes, so a hidden profile never keeps an old pair's heatmap alive) and the print store (`applyAggressiveProfilePrints`), repaints the primitive when the store says the recording changed, attaches the pane while the indicator is on and the chart is live and detaches it otherwise, and renders the pane from what the primitive reads; the store's live prints reach the pane's live bar only at or above the profile's floor, so a raised floor keeps the noise out of the forming candle too; a timeframe or symbol change empties the pane and gives the primitive a fresh source before the chart places the new view, so the old timeframe's points never stretch the time scale. `MainPanel` keeps the settings per instrument, the legend row and the dialog; the legend's detail says "loading the view's prints" while the store still has stretches of the candles on screen to ask (its `coverage` over the view, checked at every want), "some of the view's prints are missing" when it holds stretches of the view open that it will not ask now (a stretch given up for `RANGE_GIVE_UP_MS`, or a view past `MAX_RECORDED_PRINTS`), whose bars come from the heatmap's columns alone, and "the candles on screen" once it holds them; while the indicator is on it hands the chart the store and, every `RANGE_POLL_MS`, sets the store's want (the last `RANGE_WARM_MS` (7 days, what the host keeps) up to now, widened to the range on screen when that reaches further, with the range on screen, to the last visible bar's close, as the focus, so the view fills first and the rest of the week warms up behind it, nearest the view first; the store's floor, or the heatmap's until it has one; the slider never reaches the store) and asks the hook to send its next request, and drops the want when the indicator goes off or hides. `useMarketData` holds the store per pair, feeds it every live trade and the snapshot's trades as seen, routes the replies to its own request ids to it and never to Aggressive Orders' tape (which thins history older than four hours to four prints a minute a side), and sends a request only over an open socket while Aggressive Orders waits for no reply and has no retry coming, however long that takes: the host answers only the newest `aggressive_history` request per connection and drops a superseded one's reply without a frame, so the store never takes the channel on a timer, a request Aggressive Orders sends makes the store ask its page again (`lost`), and the moment Aggressive Orders' reply lands the hook asks the store's next page at once rather than on the next poll. The wait ends with that reply, whatever it says, or with a closed socket, and never starts for a request made while the socket was not open, which the worker drops.
