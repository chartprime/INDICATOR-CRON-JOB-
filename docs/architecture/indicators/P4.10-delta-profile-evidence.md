# Delta Profile (P4.10)

> **Historical implementation evidence.** This dated page records the result
> of a completed build or audit. It is not a current deployment/health
> statement; use the handbook and final-state overview for supported operation.

Delta Profile is a **computation-and-visualization tool**. It shows the
net delta (buy volume minus sell volume) of traded volume at each price
level over a period, from the same real L1 trade prints Volume Profile
uses — not a separate computation, a signed second view of one dataset. It
carries no score, gate, or trading signal.

> **Changed 2026-10-03 — Delta Profile is a view, not an indicator.** Volume
> Profile, Net Delta Profile and Delta Profile are one registered indicator
> now, `sessionVolumeProfile` / "Session Volume Profile (Volume / Net Delta /
> Delta)", with a **View**
> dropdown selecting `volumeProfile`, `netDeltaProfile` or `deltaProfile`.
> Everything this page says about the rendering, the derived levels and the
> bin values still holds for the `deltaProfile` view; what is gone is its own
> registration, its bespoke `DeltaProfileSettingsModal`, and its separate
> palette — the palette is shared by all three views now (defaults are
> Volume Profile's former colours), and a workspace saved before the merge is
> read through to the merged id once. See
> [P4.09](P4.09-volume-profile-evidence.md#session-volume-profile-one-indicator-three-views)
> for the merged indicator's contract, palette and migration rules.

> **Changed 2026-09-27 — signed net-delta bins.** The indicator previously
> drew two bars per bucket (buy right, sell left). It now draws **one signed
> net-delta bar per bucket** (`buy_volume - sell_volume`): net buying extends
> right of the zone's left edge, net selling left. The settings shape, the
> wire type, and the session/binning config were all unchanged by that change
> — it was a rendering and level-derivation change only. The two-bar
> description below is superseded by this section.

- `apps/web/src/lib/indicators/volume-profile/profilePeriodGeometry.ts` — period placement and

  developing-opacity, per-candle-on-top routing, bar-width budget capping
- `apps/web/src/lib/indicators/delta-profile/deltaProfilePrimitive.ts` — signed net-delta bar/line

  `sessionProfileSettings.ts` — the registration that carries this view, and
  the mapping from its one settings object onto this primitive (mode/period/

  the level toggles). The bespoke `DeltaProfileSettingsModal.tsx` and the
  separate `deltaProfileIndicator.ts` registration this page originally
  described were deleted by the 2026-10-03 merge.
- `apps/web/src/components/chart/MainPanel.tsx` — indicator registration, legend
  row, view/session state with the other views (now
  `runtimeSettingsById.sessionVolumeProfile`, not local `useState`)
- `apps/web/src/components/chart/ChartPanel.tsx` — primitive attach/detach, live
  and historical profile application (all three views share the same data
  refs and fetch/poll wiring; only the selected view's primitive is given
  the frame)

Delta Profile has no backend files of its own. It reads the same
`ProfileFrame` wire type `P4.09`'s engine already produces — see
`P4.09-volume-profile-evidence.md` for the engine, storage, and rollup
cache the view depends on.

## Product contract

The same three modes as the volume view, selected in the one settings
modal, since all three are views of one underlying computation:

1. **Continuous** — a recurring period (5m to 1 week) that resets on its
   own clock boundary.
2. **Per-Candle** — one profile per chart candle, aligned to the candle's
   own open/close.
3. **Fixed Range** — a one-off, user-anchored window, placed with the
   Drawing Tools "Fixed Range" tool.

Mode and period are **one settings object for all three views**, not
independent per view — changing the period in the modal changes it for
whichever view is selected and for the others, matching the single
underlying dataset they render. One view is drawn at a time, selected by
the View dropdown; the palette and the POC/VAH-VAL line toggles are shared
by all three (see P4.09 for the field-to-view mapping).

**Historical rendering is always on, no separate toggle** — the same
behaviour as Volume Profile. Whenever the indicator is enabled and
visible, every epoch-aligned period in the chart's visible range renders
as its own signed-bar zone, live period on the right edge, closed periods
to its left.

## Rendering contract

- Each price-level bucket renders as **one signed bar of its own net
  delta**, `buy_volume - sell_volume`:
  - a positive delta grows **rightward** from the zone's left edge (the
    zero line), in the buy colour;
  - a negative delta grows **leftward** from it, in the sell colour.
- A bar's length is `|delta| / maxSideVolume` against the zone's **full**
  bar-width budget, where `maxSideVolume` is the largest single side's
  volume anywhere in that profile (`max(buy_volume, sell_volume)`). One
  shared scale per profile keeps bars comparable bucket to bucket; note
  the scale base is still a side volume, not the max |delta|.
- **Value-area bins are more intense than the rest.** Buy and sell each carry
  a base colour and a brighter value-area variant; a bucket inside the delta
  value area draws in the brighter variant _and_ in the vivid, near-opaque
  opacity band (alpha ≈ 0.8–1.0), while a bucket outside it draws in the base
  colour and the dim band (alpha ≈ 0.35–0.7). Both bands still grade linearly
  with the bin's `|delta|`, so neither region goes flat. This matches Net Delta
  Profile's own treatment.
- A bucket whose buy and sell volumes net to **zero draws no bar at all** —
  not a zero-length sliver.
- Each bar prints its own **signed net delta** inside it, aligned to the bar's
  **outside edge** — left for a net-buy bar, right for a net-sell bar — and
  vertically centred, auto-sized to fit the box and scaling with zoom. A box
  too small to hold the text legibly draws none; the text colour is
  auto-contrasted against the bin fill.
- **Every profile draws above the candle series** (an overlay), in all three
  modes, so an opaque candle body never hides a bin.
- **Bars paint above the POC/VAH/VAL lines.** Both live at the top zOrder, so
  the line pane view is registered before the (on-top) bar view —
  lightweight-charts paints same-zOrder views in registration order.
- Because the zero line is the zone's own left edge, a net-sell bar reaches
  **left of the zone**, up to the full bar budget. This is accepted and
  documented rather than capped: it is the same neighbour-overlap the
  previous split-bar design already had on its sell half.
- POC and value area are **derived from |delta| client-side**
  (`deltaProfileLevels.ts`), never read from the frame: the wire's
  `poc_price`/`vah_price`/`val_price` describe gross volume. The POC is the
  bucket with the largest `|delta|`; the value area expands outward over
  `|delta|` to the target share of the profile's total `|delta|`. The share is
  the **"Value area percent"** setting (0.1–1.0 in 0.05 steps, default 0.70),
  so raising it both extends the VAH/VAL lines and repaints in/out membership.
- POC line: solid while a period is closed, dashed while it is still
  developing — same rule as Volume Profile.
- VAH/VAL lines: each bounded to its own period, drawn in a **user-selected
  style** — solid, dashed (the default), or dotted. When the value area
  collapses to a single price (VAH equals VAL), only one line is drawn, not
  two overlapping ones.
- The POC label carries the bucket's **signed** net delta (e.g. `+16`,
  `-36`), so a net-sell POC reads negative rather than as a bare magnitude.
- The chart labels read "Δ POC", "Δ VAH" and "Δ VAL", and the settings name
  them "Delta POC" and "Delta VAH / VAL", so a bare "POC" on this terminal
  always means the volume POC.
- In-bin ink is near-black or white, whichever has the higher WCAG contrast
  against the bin's fill composited over the chart's own background.
- A developing (still-open) period renders every bar and line at reduced
  opacity; a closed period renders at full opacity.
- Every mode's bars and lines render above the candle series, the same routing
  rule Volume Profile uses.
- **No single-print highlighting.** Single-print is a total-volume
  concept with no clean delta analogue, scoped out explicitly rather
  than silently dropped.
- **No POC-bucket bar highlight.** The POC is shown via its own line, but
  not reinforced with a bar fill. A deliberate, known simplification.

## Provider provenance and limits

Inherited unchanged from `P4.09` — see that document's own section for
per-provider tick-size sourcing and buy/sell aggressor-side
classification. Delta Profile renders the same `buckets[].buy_volume`/
`sell_volume` fields Volume Profile's engine already computes; it adds no
new sourcing of its own.

## Verification obligations

Deterministic tests must prove: one signed bar per nonzero-delta bucket
(net buy right of the zone's left edge, net sell left); bar length
`|delta| / maxSideVolume` over the full bar budget; a zero-net bucket draws
nothing; the buy hue for positive delta and the sell hue for negative; an
in-value-area bin brighter in both hue and opacity band than an equal-|delta|
bucket outside it; the
POC and value area derived from `|delta|` (largest `|delta|`, expanding to
the chosen value-area percent of total `|delta|`), explicitly **not** the
frame's volume-based
`poc_price`/`vah_price`/`val_price`; the VAH/VAL line style setting reaching
the canvas as the right dash array for each of solid/dashed/dotted; the
POC dashed-when-developing / solid-when-closed rule; bar-fill opacity dimming
while developing; a single
VAH/VAL line (not two) when the value area collapses to one price; a signed
POC label; enabling both Volume Profile and Delta Profile together does not
cause the backend to track two independent periods for the same instrument
(confirmed by direct source read of the backend's per-pair state, not
assumed).
